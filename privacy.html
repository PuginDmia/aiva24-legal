Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 9, 2026

1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy describes how the AIVA24 service (hereinafter — "Operator" or "we") collects, uses, and protects personal data of its users (hereinafter — "User" or "you") in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following user data:

  • Email address.
  • User's name or nickname (if provided).
  • Authentication data from third-party services (Google, Apple).
  • Call recordings and transcriptions (stored to provide Service functionality).
  • Technical data (IP address, cookies, browser information).
  • Data from connected third-party platforms (TikTok, Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp), such as usernames, profile information, and message content — only when authorized by the User.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

Data is collected for the following purposes:

  • Providing access to Service functionality.
  • Establishing communication with the User.
  • Improving service quality and developing new features.
  • Personalizing user experience.
  • Managing integrations with third-party platforms on behalf of the User.

4. Data Security

The Operator takes all necessary organizational and technical measures to protect personal data from unauthorized or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying, and distribution.

All data is stored on secure servers with encryption in transit and at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to third parties without your consent, except as required by applicable law (e.g., in response to lawful requests by public authorities) or as necessary to provide the Service through our service providers (e.g., hosting providers, communication gateways) acting on our behalf.

When integrating with third-party platforms (such as TikTok, Instagram, Telegram), we access only the data and permissions explicitly authorized by the User through the platform's OAuth flow. This data is used solely for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Users may request deletion of their data at any time by contacting our support team.

7. User Rights

The User has the right to obtain information regarding the processing of their personal data, as well as the right to request correction, blocking, or deletion of their personal data if it is incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate.

Users may disconnect any third-party platform integrations at any time through their account settings. Upon disconnection, we will cease processing data from that platform.

8. Policy Changes

The Operator reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy. The updated version will indicate the date of the last modification. Continued use of the Service after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Information

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at support@aiva24.io.
